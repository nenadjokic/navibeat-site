Karaoke lyrics

Real word and syllable timing from the OpenSubsonic lyrics extension, with instrumental-break dots. It falls back to line highlighting when a track has no word timing.

Whole-file downloads, a metadata cache, an artwork cache and an Offline Mode that never touches the network.

For self-hosters

Built for the server you already run.

NaviBeat is only a client. There is no NaviBeat account, no backend, no analytics and no tracking. The app talks to your server and to nobody else, unless you connect Last.fm or ListenBrainz yourself.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I try NaviBeat for Android without my own server?

Yes. Point the app at the official Navidrome demo: server https://demo.navidrome.org, user demo, password demo. The app itself is $5.99 on Google Play.

Does NaviBeat for Android work with Airsonic or Gonic?

Yes. Any OpenSubsonic-compatible server works, including Airsonic-Advanced and Gonic, as well as Navidrome.

Is NaviBeat on Android the same app as on iPhone?

It is written natively for Android with the same library, offline downloads and word-by-word lyrics. It is a separate purchase from the Apple apps.

Does the app track me?

No. There is no NaviBeat account, no backend, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing leaves your device except requests to your own server, and to Last.fm or ListenBrainz if you connect them yourself.

How much does NaviBeat for Android cost?

$5.99, once, on Google Play. It is a separate purchase from the Apple apps, and there is no subscription.

Does NaviBeat work on Android TV and Android Auto?

Yes. One app covers the phone and Android TV, and Android Auto lets you browse and play in the car: Shuffle All, Favourites, Recently Played, Albums, Artists, Playlists and Radio.

What music server does NaviBeat for Android need?

NaviBeat is a client, so it connects to your own server. It works with Navidrome v0.50 or later, or any OpenSubsonic server, including Airsonic-Advanced and Gonic.

Can I listen offline on Android?

Yes. Download whole files, and an Offline Mode keeps the app from touching the network. Metadata and artwork are cached on the phone.

Which Android version do I need?

Android 8.0 or newer.

Does NaviBeat scrobble to Last.fm or ListenBrainz from Android?

No. Plays go to your own server. Last.fm and ListenBrainz are read-only extras that add detail to the app.
