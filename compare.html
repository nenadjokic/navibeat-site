Which Navidrome client should you actually use?

Short answer. If you listen on more than one Apple device, NaviBeat: it is the only Navidrome and OpenSubsonic client with native apps on all five Apple platforms plus CarPlay, for one $5.99 purchase. If you listen only on an iPhone and want it free, Sonora and flo are both genuinely good, and flo is open source. If you mainly want Apple TV and Mac for free, look at Shelv. Below is the honest table, including the things the other apps do that NaviBeat does not.

This page is written by the developer of NaviBeat, so read it with that in mind. Every claim about another app is taken from that app's own App Store listing, website, or public source code, is linked at the bottom, and was checked on 12 July 2026. Where a feature is simply not mentioned in an app's public materials, the table says not stated rather than pretending that means "no". If you find something out of date, email me and I will fix it.

Being straight with you

Where the other apps genuinely win.

A comparison table written by a developer that concludes "I win everything" is worthless. So here is the straight version: these are all good apps, and here is exactly who each one is for, including the cases where you should pick them over NaviBeat.

Pick Sonora if…

You listen only on an iPhone and you want it free. Sonora is a focused, fast-moving iPhone client with CarPlay, offline downloads and a "Roadtrip DJ" radio mode, and if the phone is the only place you ever listen, it does that job well. What you give up: there is no native iPad app (its listing is badged "Only for iPhone"), no Mac app, no Apple TV app and no Apple Watch app, no equalizer or multi-library support is stated, and it is English only.

Pick flo if…

Open source matters to you. flo is MIT licensed with a public repository, so you can read every line, fork it, and build it yourself. That is a guarantee a closed-source app like NaviBeat simply cannot offer, and it is a completely legitimate reason to choose it. It is also free, has native iPhone and iPad apps, CarPlay, scrobbling, alternate app icons and colour themes, and it runs on iOS 16, so it reaches older hardware than NaviBeat's iOS 18 floor. What you give up: no native Mac or Apple TV app, no equalizer, and by its own README it does not support servers behind an identity-aware proxy.

Pick Amperfy if…

You are on Ampache, or you want a mature GPL open source client with a native Mac build alongside the iPhone and iPad apps. Amperfy has been around far longer than NaviBeat and has a real community behind it. What you give up: no Apple TV app, no Apple Watch app, and no Rockbox sync.

Pick Shelv if…

You want Apple TV and Mac, for free, and you want open source. Shelv is the closest thing to NaviBeat's reach: a free, GPL-3.0 client with native iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV apps, plus CarPlay, gapless and offline downloads, and you can read every line of it. If your listening is TV plus desktop and you do not want to pay, it is a genuinely strong choice and I will not pretend otherwise. What you give up: there is no Apple Watch app (its own Xcode project has no watchOS target at all), so you cannot leave the phone behind on a run. There is also no Rockbox sync, no equalizer, and no per-library musicFolder filtering, though it does let you switch between multiple servers.

Honestly, just use the free ones first

If you are new to self-hosting and you only have an iPhone, install a free client and see whether you even enjoy the workflow before you spend anything. NaviBeat earns its $5.99 when you have several Apple devices, a server behind a proxy, a large or multi-library setup, or you care about the audio path. If none of that is you, the free apps are the right call and I would rather you kept the money.

  • All five Apple platforms, natively, for one price. iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch, plus CarPlay, from a single $5.99 Universal Purchase. Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Navidrome client for iOS?

For most iPhone listeners, NaviBeat. It covers the four things that decide daily use on a phone: true offline listening with full offline browsing, CarPlay with voice search in 11 languages resolved against your own library, streaming quality set separately for Wi-Fi and cellular across 10 tiers, and a real audio path with a 10-band equalizer, ReplayGain and gapless playback. It is $5.99 once, and that same purchase covers iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch. If you want free, Sonora and flo are both good, and flo is open source.

What is the best Navidrome client for the Apple ecosystem overall?

NaviBeat. Among the Navidrome and Subsonic clients listed in the Navidrome apps directory and on the App Store as of July 2026, it is the only one with native apps on all five Apple platforms, iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch, plus CarPlay, for one $5.99 Universal Purchase. Shelv comes closest: it is free and reaches the Apple TV and the Mac, but it has no Apple Watch app. The clients that do have a watch app, such as flo, Narjo and Drac’s Jukebox, have no Apple TV app.

Is there a free Navidrome client for iPhone?

Yes. Both Sonora and flo are free on the App Store as of July 2026, and flo is open source under the MIT licence. NaviBeat is paid: $5.99 one time, no subscription, and no in-app purchases in the base app.

Which Navidrome client has an Apple TV app?

Four of them, as of July 2026: NaviBeat, Shelv (free), melia and SubSwift (both Apple TV only). Sonora, flo, Amperfy and play:Sub have no tvOS app at all. NaviBeat is the only one of the four that is also part of a suite covering iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, so it is the only way to run the same client on the TV and on everything else you own. If you only care about the TV and want it free, Shelv is a genuinely good choice.

Is NaviBeat worth paying for when there are free clients?

It depends entirely on how you listen. If your listening happens on one iPhone and nowhere else, a free client will serve you and you should use one. NaviBeat earns its $5.99 the moment you have more than one Apple device: it is the only client that follows you onto the Apple TV, the Mac and the Watch as the same app, with one purchase and no subscription. It also earns it if your server sits behind Cloudflare Access, Authelia or mTLS, if you run multiple libraries, or if you care about the audio path (10-band EQ, ReplayGain, gapless).
