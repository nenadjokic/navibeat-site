The short answer

Why NaviBeat is the best Navidrome client for Mac.

Short answer. Most Navidrome clients you can install on a Mac are the iPhone or iPad binary running on Apple Silicon. NaviBeat is a real, native Mac app built in SwiftUI: not Electron, not Catalyst, not a phone app in a window. It has a Mini Player that pins on top of any window, a menu-bar presence, desktop widgets, and it is the only Navidrome client that can sync your library to a Rockbox iPod from a normal Mac interface instead of a terminal script.

01 / Actually native

A Mac app, not a phone app on a Mac.

Written in SwiftUI for macOS 15. Real window management that remembers its size and position, a sidebar, keyboard control, directory browsing, and a menu-bar icon that stays put. The Mini Player pins on top of whatever you are working in. There is a 7-day free trial on the Mac App Store.

02 / Rockbox iPod sync

NaviSyncRock: your Navidrome, on a real iPod.

A one-time $4.99 in-app unlock, shipped in 1.3.0. Right-click a song, album, artist, or playlist and send it to a Rockbox player: your own server does the transcoding, album art is re-encoded so old hardware renders it, .m3u8 playlists are written correctly, plays made on the device sync back to Navidrome, and two custom NaviBeat iPod themes are installed. No terminal, no Python scripts. See how it works.

03 / Audio quality

A desktop-grade audio path.

A selectable high-fidelity playback engine with a 10-band equalizer and presets, ReplayGain (track, album, or automatic) so a shuffle across a mixed library does not swing wildly in loudness, and gapless playback for live albums and mixes. Ten streaming-quality tiers, or send the original file untouched.

04 / Homelab-grade access

It reaches the server you actually built.

Custom HTTP headers for Cloudflare Access and Authelia, mTLS client certificates, and access over Tailscale, with no third-party relay in between. Many clients only speak to a plain, directly exposed server. NaviBeat is built for the one behind your proxy.

Every screen

A native Mac app that looks and behaves like one.

Sidebar navigation, keyboard shortcuts, menu bar mini player, drag-and-drop queue reordering. Every detail of the macOS platform considered: not a port, not a compromise. Downloads now keep a retryable history with live speed and queue stats, and Prefer Downloaded Music, in Settings, keeps local copies playing from disk even when you are back online.

01 / Home

Sidebar, Featured Album, Recently Played.

The sidebar gives you instant access to Home, Artists, Albums, Playlists, Radio, and Directories. The main area opens on a Featured Album hero with your recently played albums below. Connect Last.fm or ListenBrainz in Settings and Home adds a personalized stats shelf too.

02 / Mini Player

Compact. Always visible. Pin-on-top.

Switch to the Mini Player and it floats above every other window, even full-screen video. Album art, track name, transport controls. Resize it to taste. One keyboard shortcut to show or hide it.

03 / Time-Synced Lyrics

Side panel or full screen, your call.

Open the lyrics panel alongside your library, or expand it to fill the window. Drop an .lrc file next to your track or let NaviBeat pull synced lyrics from your server automatically.

04 / Albums

Your whole library at a glance.

Albums grid with cover art, artist name, release year, and genre. Sort by recently added, alphabetically, by artist, or by year. Click any album for the full track list with Hi-Res badges.

05 / Internet Radio

Navidrome radio stations, natively on Mac.

Internet radio stations configured in Navidrome appear in the Radio sidebar item. One click to start a stream. Station name and now-playing metadata show in the toolbar and Mini Player.

06 / Classical

Classical, done right.

Albums tagged as classical group by work on Mac too, with movements listed in order beneath each one. A suite stays one work instead of several unrelated tracks. Needs a Navidrome 0.63+ server for the work and movement tags.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I install NaviBeat on my Mac?

Open the Mac App Store, search for NaviBeat, and install it. It runs on macOS 15 or later, on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

What music server does NaviBeat need?

NaviBeat is a client, so it connects to your own server. It works with Navidrome v0.50 or later, or any OpenSubsonic-compatible server, including Airsonic-Advanced and Gonic. It does not host or stream any music of its own.

How much does it cost, and does one purchase cover the Mac?

NaviBeat is $5.99 USD, one time. It is a Universal Purchase, so that single price covers all five Apple platforms: Mac, iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, and Apple Watch.

Is it a real Mac app or a scaled-up iPad app?

It is a native Mac app that looks and behaves like one, not a scaled-up iPad build.
