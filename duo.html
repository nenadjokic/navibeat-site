Folded

Still all yours, in one hand.

The outer screen runs the whole app: Home, your library, playlists, downloads and Now Playing. The tab bar sits on the edge, where your thumb is. Hi-Res and lossless from your own server, folded or not.

Open

Half open like a book, Now Playing splits on the hinge itself, so nothing sits in the fold. Album and artist pages keep that split even when the Duo lies flat.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does NaviBeat work on iPhone Duo?

Yes. NaviBeat uses the outer screen when the Duo is folded and both pages when it is open. Now Playing, album pages and artist pages split on the hinge, so one page never covers the other.

When does the Duo layout reach the App Store?

Since version 1.5.4, on the App Store.

Do I need a separate purchase for iPhone Duo?

No. NaviBeat is $5.99 USD once, as a Universal Purchase. The same purchase covers iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch.
