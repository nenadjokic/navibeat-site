A Navidrome client for every device.

Navidrome keeps your music. A client plays it. Here is NaviBeat on each platform, what it costs and where to get it.

By Nenad Jokic, who makes NaviBeat. 25 November 2026

Navidrome is a music server you run yourself. It has a web player, and it speaks the Subsonic API, so native apps can play from it with offline downloads, lock screen controls and car support. Those apps are the clients. NaviBeat is one client that runs on all of these:

  • NaviBeat for iPhone: iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch, plus CarPlay. One $5.99 Universal Purchase on the App Store.
  • NaviBeat for Android: Android phone, Android TV and Android Auto. $5.99 once on Google Play.
  • NaviBeat for Windows: Windows 10 and 11. Five days free, then $9.99 once, one key for three computers.
  • NaviBeat for Linux: Linux. Free: AppImage, apt, dnf and pacman.

Your hearts, ratings, playlists and play counts live on your Navidrome server, so every device shows the same library. NaviBeat works with Navidrome, Airsonic-Advanced, Gonic and any OpenSubsonic server.

What a native client adds

Offline downloads for the car and the plane, time-synced lyrics, gapless playback, an equalizer, CarPlay and Android Auto, widgets, and the lock screen and remote controls each system expects. The full list is on Features, and the sound options are in Sound in NaviBeat.

Which iPhone client?

If you only need Apple devices and want to see the other Navidrome apps for iPhone side by side, read the sourced comparison. Navidrome also keeps its own list of apps.

Getting started

Install NaviBeat, type your server address, your username and your password. The steps, and what to do when the server does not answer, are in Connect NaviBeat to Navidrome. To listen away from home without opening a port, see Navidrome remote access.

Questions

Is there a Navidrome client for every platform?

Yes. NaviBeat runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch with CarPlay, on Android phones, Android TV and Android Auto, on Windows 10 and 11, and on Linux.

How much does NaviBeat cost?

On Apple it is one $5.99 Universal Purchase for every Apple device. On Android it is $5.99 once on Google Play. On Windows it is five days free, then $9.99 once. On Linux it is free. Each store is its own purchase.

Does NaviBeat work with servers other than Navidrome?

Yes. It works with Navidrome, Airsonic-Advanced, Gonic and any OpenSubsonic server.

Does NaviBeat need an account or send my data anywhere?

No NaviBeat account, no analytics and no tracking. The app talks to the server you run. It reaches Last.fm, ListenBrainz and lrclib.net only if you switch them on, and musicbrainz.org and coverartarchive.org for the New Releases shelf on Home, which you can switch off.
