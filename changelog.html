NaviBeat / Changelog

What changed in each version.

The App Store versions of NaviBeat for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch, newest first, in plain words.

By Nenad Jokic, who makes NaviBeat. 14 November 2026

One purchase covers iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch. Every version below went out on the App Store. The notes follow the iPhone and iPad release notes. A line that starts with Mac is for the Mac. Newer builds reach the TestFlight beta first, almost every day. What is planned is on the roadmap.

In the beta now

These are in the TestFlight beta as 1.5.11 and can still change before they reach the App Store.

  • Favorite radio stations. Long press a station and tap Add to Favorites. Favorites get a star, go to the top of Radios and come first in Home's Radio section.
  • A moon button next to ... in Now Playing starts the sleep timer.
  • Siri on iPhone knows the names of your playlists and artists, so it hears them right when you ask NaviBeat to play one.
  • Continue on Home brings a shuffled playlist back shuffled.
  • Classical albums keep the full work name as the heading.
  • After the phone wakes, a UPnP speaker plays the next song.

1.5.10

November 2026

  • Released on This Day: a new Home shelf with albums that came out on this date in earlier years.
  • With the equalizer on, crossfade no longer jumps in volume at the start or cuts the end of a song.
  • Play on an album page continues from the last song you heard. A finished album starts again from the first song.
  • UPnP and DLNA speakers: NaviBeat follows a speaker that moves to the next song by itself, and takes over a speaker that already plays a song from your queue. The Playing on bar has the speaker volume and a speaker menu.
  • A Favorite Artists shelf on Home. A heart you set while the server cannot be reached is saved at once.
  • Apple Watch with a server behind a client certificate: the iPhone gives the certificate to the watch, so the watch can browse and download.
  • On mobile data, songs ahead download only after the playing song has run 20 seconds, so skipping no longer downloads whole songs.
  • Artists can be filtered by song count. All Songs on an artist remembers the last song and asks Continue or Start Over.
  • Copy Share Link in an album's menu.
  • Mac: Library Doctor finds your files on a Navidrome server with more than one library. The Mac no longer freezes when a slow network share is mounted.

1.5.9

November 2026

  • NaviBeat no longer closes right after it opens on the early iOS 27.2 and macOS 27.2 betas.
  • Play on the Songs screen starts at once and keeps adding the rest of your library behind the songs on screen.
  • If the radio was the last thing playing, it comes back when you get in the car.
  • Library Statistics: albums by decade, songs per genre, and how much of your library you have played.
  • One address at home and away (split DNS): NaviBeat knows when you are home, so your home quality applies and Only away from home can be switched on.
  • A song already at or below your Download quality downloads untouched.
  • Crossfade works with Gapless playback off. ReplayGain raises quiet songs on the AudioStreaming engine.
  • Song Path to Here, in the menu of any song: a path from the song that is playing to that one.
  • CarPlay: an album has Go to Artist, the artist page starts with Top Songs, and Home has a row of random albums you can refresh.
  • Radio stations that stream Opus play again.
  • Mac: the mini player on radio has Pin Window On Top, and Open Station Website when the station has one.

1.5.8

November 2026

  • Mix with Playlist, in a playlist's menu, plays two playlists one song at a time.
  • Sync Settings carries your settings through iCloud to iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. Audio engine, quality per network, downloads and sign-in stay on each device.
  • Play from Playlist in the song menu starts a playlist from that song. Show Artist lists every artist when a song credits several.
  • With no connection, Home adds Recently Added, Favorites and Recently Played from your downloads, and saved radio stations work without the server.
  • Show quality in album grids, a new switch in Settings.
  • With a keyboard: Up and Down change the volume, Left and Right seek.
  • Fixed: freezes while covers load, a crash on a recent search in CarPlay, and seeking in a streamed FLAC.
  • Mac: Artists, Albums and Songs have an A-Z rail, and Playlists has a Filter field. Library Doctor lets you pick which copy of a duplicate album to keep.

1.5.7

October 2026

  • Play on, Control and Send to. Your iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV see each other on your Wi-Fi. Control another NaviBeat from this one, or send your queue to it and it goes on from the same second. How it works.
  • Keep library on this device, in Settings, Library & Data, saves your library list and covers. With it on, Offline Mode shows the whole library and greys out what is not downloaded.
  • Add Account on This Server, for a second user on the same server. Share Settings sends your settings as a link or QR code.
  • Home: My Playlist Rows you name and fill yourself, a Pinned Playlists row, Radio Stations, and "N new" on Recently Added.
  • Play the Artist's Next Album when an album ends. AirPlay to several speakers at once.
  • Lyrics: background singers, Furigana and Reading for Japanese, and Flag Lyrics for wrong lyrics.
  • Apple Watch: double tap plays and pauses (Series 9, Ultra 2 and later), and lyrics show for streamed songs.
  • On a classic Subsonic server the Songs list fills, and search finds part of a word.
  • NaviBeat now speaks Catalan.

1.5.5

October 2026

  • Popular songs: a flame in front of the track number marks up to three popular songs on each album. Settings, Popular songs picks the source: your server, Last.fm, ListenBrainz or Off.
  • The Now Playing widget works away from home and in Offline Mode, using your downloads.
  • Now Playing on This Server, a Home shelf with what others on your server are playing. It is off until you turn it on in Customise Home.
  • Listening Stats work without a Last.fm account and show what is most played on your server.
  • Shuffle never starts on the song that is already playing.
  • Crossfade leaves songs played in order from one album alone. Shuffled playlists still crossfade.
  • Refresh Lyrics in the song menu asks your server again. Airsonic Advanced servers connect.
  • Mac: writing Library Doctor fixes is a one-time unlock in Settings, In-App Purchases. The check and Undo stay free.

1.5.4

October 2026

  • iPhone Duo. Folded, the whole app is on the outer screen. Half open, Now Playing splits on the hinge: cover and controls on the left page, lyrics or Up Next on the right. See it on the Duo.
  • Add to Playlist shows which playlists already have a song. Check or uncheck, then Save.
  • Shuffle Mix can let you choose the first song.
  • Radio Now Playing works like the song screen, with the same backdrop and cover styles.
  • From a genre, an artist or album shows only that genre's songs.
  • Fade on pause and play, in Settings, Playback, off by default.
  • A downloaded playlist picks up songs added on the server.
  • Files the device cannot decode, such as APE, are converted by the server.
  • Mac: Library Doctor, a new sidebar item that checks your library for split albums, wrong track numbers, missing covers and duplicate imports. You review every change before it is written, and every write can be undone.
  • Mac: View, Bigger Text and Smaller Text, from 85 to 150 percent.

1.5.3

October 2026

  • CarPlay without signal: when your server does not answer within 5 seconds, albums, playlists, Favorites, artists and genres come from your downloads.
  • When a radio station announces a song, the song's own cover replaces the station logo, on the lock screen and in CarPlay too.
  • Recently Played on Home.
  • Add to Playlist shows your three most recent playlists on top.
  • A server switcher on iPhone and iPad when you have two or more servers saved.
  • Same user, new address: NaviBeat asks whether to bring over your offline playlists, downloads and library choice.
  • Show on MusicBrainz in the album, artist and song menus, when your server has the MusicBrainz id.
  • Mac: Direct Playback sets your output device to each song's sample rate when the device supports it. The Songs screen can be a table, sorted by any column.

1.5.2

September 2026

  • Ready for Navidrome's new sign-in. NaviBeat tries the token first, and if your server says no, it signs in with your password and remembers that for the server. It can also sign in with an API key.
  • Artist and album pages and Now Playing can take the blurred colour of the cover.
  • Turn your iPhone sideways and an album or artist page is two columns: cover on the left, songs on the right.
  • If songs you added with Play Next or Add to Queue are still waiting, a tap on a song asks before it replaces them.
  • The equalizer reads AutoEQ: import a headphone profile from text, use peak and shelf bands, and drag points on the curve.
  • Smart playlists show as tiles above your playlists.
  • Downloaded playlists keep the server's order offline.
  • Apple TV works behind a gateway that needs custom headers, covers included.
  • Mac: choose where downloads live, an external drive too. Stop is on Cmd+period.
  • Apple Watch runs from watchOS 10.

Every version also carries fixes that are too small to list. For the full feature list, see features.
