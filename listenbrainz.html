NaviBeat / ListenBrainz

Open · Opt-in · You’re in control

The open ListenBrainz integration, done right.

TL;DR. ListenBrainz is the open, self-hostable listening service from MetaBrainz, the same nonprofit behind the MusicBrainz music database. Connecting it in NaviBeat is opt-in. NaviBeat never submits listens to ListenBrainz: your plays go to your own Navidrome server, and it forwards them only if you set that up. The one thing NaviBeat writes back is a love when you star a track, and you can switch that off. NaviBeat reads ListenBrainz data for a Your ListenBrainz Home shelf, Your Mixes (Weekly & Daily Jams), Show on ListenBrainz, and star-first loved-track sync. Prefer Last.fm, or want the deeper Year-in-Review and Time Machine stats? See the Last.fm page.

A Your ListenBrainz stats shelf on Home. Your Mixes: fresh Weekly and Daily Jam playlists pulled straight from your ListenBrainz account. Show on ListenBrainz from any track, album, or artist menu. Loved tracks synced both ways with your Navidrome stars, star-first, so unstarring a track never wrongly erases a love that still exists on ListenBrainz. ListenBrainz with NaviBeat: four guardrails

We never send your listens

NaviBeat does not submit listens to ListenBrainz. Not once, not ever. Your plays go to your own Navidrome server, and ListenBrainz hears about them only if you told your server to forward them. What NaviBeat sends ListenBrainz directly is one thing: when you star a track, it loves that track (you can switch that off). Everything else it does with ListenBrainz is reading.

Fully optional

Don’t want ListenBrainz? Don’t connect it. NaviBeat works completely without it: same player, same library, zero external requests. Connecting ListenBrainz is one guided flow in Settings.

Sign in once, sync everywhere

Connect ListenBrainz on iPhone, and iPad, Mac, Apple TV, and Apple Watch all pick up the session via iCloud Keychain. Apple TV pairs with a QR code plus a pasted token, since there’s no browser to sign in with.

No double-counting with Last.fm

Connect both services and the surfaces they share (listener counts, artist tags, similar artists, AutoMix) never render twice. Pick a Preferred Source in Settings → Music Data and NaviBeat uses it consistently across the app.

Open · Self-hostable · MetaBrainz

Not a brand you have to trust. An open project.

ListenBrainz is the listening-history and recommendation service built by MetaBrainz, the nonprofit that also runs MusicBrainz, the open, community-maintained music database. Where Last.fm is a closed commercial service, ListenBrainz is open source, free, and self-hostable: your data belongs to you, and the project’s recommendations are built on MusicBrainz’s open metadata rather than a proprietary catalog. NaviBeat treats it as a first-class, independent connection, not an afterthought bolted onto the Last.fm integration.

What ListenBrainz adds on its own

  • ✓ Your ListenBrainz Home shelf: your listen count, top artists, and top tracks, tappable straight through to the artist or track.
  • ✓ Your Mixes: ListenBrainz’s own Weekly and Daily Jam discovery playlists, resolved against your library and ready to play.
  • ✓ Show on ListenBrainz: an instant link out to the track, album, or artist page on listenbrainz.org from any context menu.
  • ✓ MusicBrainz-ID (MBID) matching: where available, tracks are resolved by their MusicBrainz identifier instead of title/artist text alone, for more accurate matching against your library.

Loved tracks, star-first

  • ✓ Two-way sync: heart a track in NaviBeat and it’s submitted as feedback to ListenBrainz through your Navidrome server; loved on ListenBrainz and it’s starred in your library on the next sync.
  • ✓ Star-first conflict policy: unstarring a track in NaviBeat never silently erases a love that still exists on ListenBrainz, avoiding the classic two-way-sync trap of one side quietly winning.
  • ✓ Independent of Last.fm: works whether or not you also connect Last.fm. Use either, both, or neither.

As with Last.fm, ListenBrainz has no concept of album-, artist-, or playlist-level favouriting, and no star-rating concept. NaviBeat’s favouriting surface stays scoped to per-song hearts and per-song ratings for the same reason on both services: those are the only signals that round-trip cleanly. Ratings stay server-only, stored on your Navidrome via the Subsonic setRating endpoint.

Shared surfaces · ListenBrainz-only

What’s shared with Last.fm, and what’s exclusively yours.

ListenBrainz doesn’t try to duplicate every Last.fm surface. A handful of features (listener counts, artist tags, similar tracks, AutoMix) are source-neutral: connect ListenBrainz, Last.fm, or both, and NaviBeat arbitrates through your Preferred Source in Settings → Music Data so nothing renders twice. A few surfaces belong to ListenBrainz alone, and a few deep-history features are still Last.fm-only today.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does NaviBeat submit listens directly to ListenBrainz?

No. NaviBeat never submits listens to ListenBrainz. Your plays go to your own Navidrome server, and ListenBrainz sees them only if you configured your server to forward them. NaviBeat writes to ListenBrainz in exactly one place: when you star a track it loves that track, and you can turn that off in Settings. Everything else it does with ListenBrainz is reading.

Do I have to connect ListenBrainz to use NaviBeat?

No. ListenBrainz is fully optional. Don't connect it and NaviBeat works exactly the same: same player, same library, zero external requests. Connecting it is one guided flow in Settings.

Can I use ListenBrainz and Last.fm at the same time?

Yes. Several features (listener counts, artist tags, similar tracks, AutoMix) are source-neutral: connect ListenBrainz, Last.fm, or both, and NaviBeat arbitrates through your Preferred Source in Settings, Music Data, so nothing renders twice.

Why ListenBrainz instead of only Last.fm?

ListenBrainz is not a brand you have to trust, it is an open project from MetaBrainz. A few surfaces belong to ListenBrainz alone, while a few deep-history features are still Last.fm-only today. This page shows a transparent roadmap of what is done, in progress, and planned.
