Roadmap

What gets built next is partly your call

Everything here started as an email from someone using NaviBeat or NaviFin. Vote for what you need and it moves up the list. No account, no sign-up, and nothing you write appears on this page until a human has read it.

  1. 1 · VoteOne press per item. Press again to take it back. Votes decide what I look at first, not automatically what I build first.
  2. 2 · Add what is missingNot on the list? Suggest it below. It lands in the same support queue as email and gets the same reply.
  3. 3 · Watch it moveItems travel from being considered, to in progress, to shipped. Things I decide against stay visible, so a vote is never quietly dropped.

Ask for something

A bug I have not seen, or a feature you keep reaching for and it is not there. This goes straight into the support queue, so you get an answer either way. It shows up on this board only once I have read it and written a public description for it.

Your email is only used to answer you. Nothing you type here is published as written.
