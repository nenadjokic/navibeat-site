# NaviBeat Wiki (full text)

> Every setting in NaviBeat, the native Navidrome / OpenSubsonic music player for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch and CarPlay, explained one by one: what it does, where it is, its default and the platforms that have it. Generated from the app's own Settings index and checked against the code. Human pages: https://navibeat.app/wiki/

## Guides

### Connect your server
URL: https://navibeat.app/wiki/guide-connect-your-server
Sign NaviBeat in to your Navidrome or any OpenSubsonic server, at home and away.
- First sign-in: Open NaviBeat. The first screen asks for your server. Type your Server URL, the address you use to open Navidrome in a browser. Optional: add an Alternative address (home network). NaviBeat uses it at home and the main address everywhere else. Sign in with your Navidrome Username and Password, or with an API key if your server accepts them. iOS asks for Local Network access. Allow it, or a server on your Wi-Fi cannot be reached. A server on the internet works without it.
- Servers behind Cloudflare Access, Authelia or mTLS: Add the headers your proxy expects under Server addresses, in Custom HTTP headers. If your proxy asks for a client certificate, import it with Import client certificate (mTLS).
- Your other devices: Sync credentials to your other Apple devices shares the login through iCloud Keychain. For an Apple TV, use Set Up Apple TV on your iPhone, so you never type on the TV.
- More than one server or account: Save each one under Saved Servers and switch in two taps. Add Account on This Server keeps the address and asks only for a login. That suits a family that shares one server.

### Offline and downloads
URL: https://navibeat.app/wiki/guide-offline-and-downloads
Download music, keep your whole library on the device, and know what plays without a network.
- Download albums and playlists: Use the Download button on an album or playlist, or the long-press menu on a song. The files are saved at your Download quality. Offline downloads in Settings shows how many songs you have and the space they take.
- Keep your whole library here: Turn on Download my music under Offline Library. New albums download on their own, only on Wi-Fi if you want, and it stops before your device gets too full. Nothing is ever deleted by it.
- Playing without a network: Prefer Downloaded Music plays your downloaded copy even when you are online. Offline Mode shows only downloaded music and pauses streaming. In the car, Offline Mode while in CarPlay can switch it on for you.
- Weak signal on the road: On cellular, Songs Kept Ready downloads the next songs while one plays, so the music keeps going through a dead spot. Keep Streamed Songs stores what you stream, so the next play comes from the device.
- Apple Watch: Songs you send to the watch use Watch Audio Quality. Show downloaded only on Apple Watch keeps the watch Library to what is on it, for runs and flights.

### Made for You and Discover
URL: https://navibeat.app/wiki/guide-made-for-you
Mixes built from your own library, your Last.fm history and ListenBrainz.
- The Made for You shelf: Home shows Discover Mix, Time Machine, Replay and Year in Review. They use your Last.fm history when Last.fm is connected, and your play history on the device otherwise. If your server runs AudioMuse-AI, sonic mixes built from how your songs sound appear on the same shelf.
- With Last.fm: Connect Last.fm in Scrobbling & Stats. NaviBeat reads your history back: similar songs, albums and artists, listener counts and your top artists. NaviBeat never scrobbles by itself. Your Navidrome forwards your plays to Last.fm when you set that up in Navidrome.
- With ListenBrainz: Connect ListenBrainz to get your Weekly Jams and Daily Jams on Home, your listen stats and loved songs in sync. It is free, and you can even point it at your own server.
- Music that never stops: AutoMix adds similar songs when your queue ends, and AutoMix seeds from picks where they come from. Play the Artist's Next Album continues an artist's discography in release order.

### CarPlay
URL: https://navibeat.app/wiki/guide-carplay
Set up what the car shows, which buttons it has, and what happens when it connects.
- Where it starts: Opens to picks the first screen when your car connects: Home, your favorites, artists, albums or downloads. Customize CarPlay Home chooses the shelves and their order, separately from your phone.
- Buttons: CarPlay Buttons picks the buttons next to the playback controls. For audiobooks and podcasts, turn on Jump Buttons and Use Them on the Lock Screen, and the car gets skip back and skip forward.
- When the car connects: Some cars start the last music app by themselves. Never start playing when the car connects stops that. Offline Mode while in CarPlay switches to downloaded music for the drive. Pause during navigation prompts pauses the music while Maps speaks and resumes a couple of seconds back.

### Handoff and Connect
URL: https://navibeat.app/wiki/guide-handoff-and-connect
Move playback between your iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV, send music to speakers, and listen together.
- Continue on another device: When NaviBeat plays on one device, the others show a Continue on This Device card on Home. Tap it and the song continues from the same second. Pause other devices on handoff stops the first device, so you do not hear it twice.
- Send music to another device: With Allow other devices to send music here on, another NaviBeat on the same network and account can send what it plays to this device.
- Network speakers: Speakers on your network sends music to a UPnP or DLNA speaker, AV receiver or smart TV. The speaker fetches the music from your server, so the server must be reachable from it.
- Listen Together: Start Listen Together and friends on the same Wi-Fi and server scan the code. They add songs, and you control playback.
- If something does not connect: Turn on the Connect and Send to channel in Troubleshooting, try again, then prepare and send the log.

### NaviSyncRock
URL: https://navibeat.app/wiki/guide-navisyncrock
Copy music from your server to a Rockbox player, from your Mac.
- What it does: Connect a Rockbox player to your Mac, and NaviSyncRock copies albums and playlists to it. Songs are converted to a format the player plays, and covers are sized for its screen.
- Getting it: NaviSyncRock is an in-app purchase on the Mac. Restore Purchases brings it back on a new Mac.
- More: The full story, supported players and themes are on the Rockbox page.

### Server addresses
URL: https://navibeat.app/wiki/server-addresses
Where: Settings > Server & Library > Server addresses
Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV
Your server's public address, an optional home network address, and custom headers. What it does. Primary server URL is your always-reachable address, like a public domain or a tunnel. It is checked with the server when you change it. Secondary server URL (home network) is an optional LAN address. On Wi-Fi, NaviBeat uses it automatically whenever it can reach it. On cellular it always uses the primary address. Custom HTTP headers are sent with every request. Use them for Cloudflare Access, Authelia or basic-auth in front of Navidrome. How to change it. Open Settings. Tap Server & Library. Find Server addresses and change it. Good to know. Settings shows Home network or Primary, so you can see which one is in use.

### Import client certificate (mTLS)
URL: https://navibeat.app/wiki/import-client-certificate-mtls
Where: Settings > Server & Library > Import client certificate (mTLS)
Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Mac
Signs in to a server that asks for a client certificate (mutual TLS). What it does. If your reverse proxy only lets in devices with a certificate, import your .p12 file and its password here. Remove certificate takes it away. How to change it. Open Settings. Tap Server & Library. Find Import client certificate (mTLS) and change it. Good to know. While a certificate is in use, playback uses the AudioStreaming engine. Quit and relaunch NaviBeat to apply a certificate change to playback.

## Playback & Audio

### Direct Playback
URL: https://navibeat.app/wiki/direct-playback
Where: Settings > Playback & Audio > Quality & Streaming > Direct Playback
Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV
Default: Off
Plays the file exactly as it is stored on your server, with nothing from NaviBeat in between. What it does. With Direct Playback on, NaviBeat asks your server for the untouched file at full size, on Wi-Fi and on cellular alike. The equalizer, ReplayGain and crossfade are turned off, and the stream quality pickers no longer apply. NaviBeat also asks iOS to play at the file's own sample rate and channel count. How far that goes depends on your output: Apple documents that playback above 48 kHz needs an external DAC, which the built-in speaker and Bluetooth do not provide. How to change it. Open Settings. Tap Playback & Audio. Tap Quality & Streaming. Find Direct Playback and change it. Good to know. Songs you already downloaded keep playing as they were downloaded, at your Download quality. A change takes effect on the next song. Direct Playback works with the System (AVPlayer) engine. The AudioStreaming (beta) engine always converts to 44.1 kHz stereo, so it cannot play a file untouched.

### Offline Mode
URL: https://navibeat.app/wiki/offline-mode
Where: Settings > Playback & Audio > Quality & Streaming > Offline Mode
Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch
Default: Off
Shows only the music you downloaded, and pauses streaming until you turn it off. What it does. Offline Mode limits NaviBeat to downloaded artists, albums, playlists and songs. Tap Quality & Streaming. Find Offline Mode and change it. Good to know. On the Apple Watch, Offline Mode is a separate switch in the watch's own Settings. Offline Mode is also available as a Shortcuts action, so an automation can turn it on for you.

### Prefer Downloaded Music
URL: https://navibeat.app/wiki/prefer-downloaded-music
Where: Settings > Playback & Audio > Quality & Streaming > Prefer Downloaded Music
Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Mac

### Auto preamp
URL: https://navibeat.app/wiki/auto-preamp

Lowers the whole equalizer by part of its biggest boost; a limiter at the output catches the rest so it does not clip. What it does. ### Keyboard Shortcuts
