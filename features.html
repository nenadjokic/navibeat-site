Every feature, one page

Everything NaviBeat does.

One app, one $5.99 purchase, five Apple platforms plus CarPlay. This is the whole list: what is on the App Store today, and what is finished and running in the beta right now.

In the beta right now

Finished, running, and one release away.

Everything below is built and in testers' hands through TestFlight today. It reaches the App Store in the next release. Anyone can join the beta, and it is free.

Beta / Appearance

Pick your own accent colour.

Amber is still the default and one tap brings it back, but the whole app now follows whatever colour you choose: six one-tap presets, a full colour picker, and a hex field if you know exactly what you want.

The text and glyphs drawn on top of your colour switch between black and white on their own, so a pale yellow stays as readable as a deep purple, and the gradient follows your choice instead of staying orange.

iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Beta / Player

Jump buttons, for the things you do not listen to end to end.

Skip back and skip forward by 10, 15 or 30 seconds, either side of Play. Next and Previous stay exactly where they are.

They can be limited to one library, which is the point if you keep audiobooks and podcasts on the same server as your music: turn them on for that library and your music player is unchanged. Off until you ask for them.

iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.

Beta / Continuity

Start on one device, carry on with another.

Leave a song half finished on the Mac and NaviBeat on your phone offers to continue it, at the second you stopped, with the rest of the queue intact. Tap it and the Mac pauses itself, within a few seconds, so you are never chased through the house by your own music.

It works through your own server's play queue, so there is nothing extra to configure and nothing leaves your network.

iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.

Beta / Playlists

Smart Playlists you write yourself.

Build a playlist out of rules rather than by hand: artist, genre, year, rating, play count, when you added it, whether it is in another playlist, and more. Results update as your library does, and you see them while you are still editing the rules.

Definitions sync across your devices, and a snapshot can be written back to your server as an ordinary playlist so every other client sees it too.

Everywhere, including Apple TV.

Beta / Offline

Offline stopped being a separate mode.

Downloaded music now lives inside the normal app. The same Home, the same artists, the same albums, with anything you do not have on the device shown greyed out instead of hidden, so you always know what is missing rather than wondering where it went.

When your server cannot be reached, NaviBeat switches to your downloads on its own and switches back when the server returns. Favourites, ratings and artwork keep working while you are cut off, and catch up afterwards.

iPhone, iPad, Mac and CarPlay.

Beta / Servers

Saved servers, and no retyping.

Keep several servers side by side and switch between them without entering anything again. Each entry carries its own proxy headers and its own mTLS client certificate, so a server behind an identity-aware proxy is a saved entry like any other.

iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Beta / Artists

A discography that behaves like a discography.

Albums are grouped by release type, so albums, EPs, singles, live records and compilations stop fighting for the same list. An All Songs view flattens an artist's whole catalogue into one sortable list, play counts show on the album page, and Top Songs are visible on the artist header rather than hidden in a menu.

Albums released in the same year are finally in the right order: if your files carry a release date, NaviBeat reads it down to the day, and an original release beats a later reissue so a box of remasters cannot jump to the front.

iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.

Beta / Lyrics

Lyrics, and an optional lookup when your server has none.

Time-synced lyrics scroll with the song and a tap on any line seeks to it. New in the beta: if your server and its own lyrics plugin both come up empty, NaviBeat can ask lrclib.net instead.

That lookup is off until you turn it on, and it sends the song title, artist, album and length. Nothing about you and nothing about your server.

Your server is always asked first.

Beta / Automation

Shortcuts, and a share link that is safe to send.

Shortcuts actions for play, pause, next, previous and shuffle, plus Play Playlist by name, so NaviBeat can sit inside your own automations and Focus modes.

Copy Share Link asks your server for a proper share URL rather than pasting anything of yours into a message.

iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.

Beta / Access

VoiceOver on every control, and Reduce Motion honoured.

Every transport control announces what it does and, where it is a toggle, what state it is in, instead of reading out the name of an icon. Reduce Motion turns off the animation the rest of the app leans on.

iPhone, iPad and Mac.

On the App Store today

Your library, the way you filed it.

Library / Multi-Library

Real Navidrome multi-library support.

All Libraries, one, or any subset you like, filtered on the server rather than hidden in the app. Music in one library and audiobooks in another stay apart everywhere: Home, search, shuffle, CarPlay and the Watch. How it works.

No other Apple client filters by musicFolder.

Library / Browsing

Albums, artists, genres, playlists, folders.

Sort and filter the way you think: by name, by artist, by year, by date added, by rating, by play count. Genres are first class, folder browsing is there for libraries organised by directory, and every tag on an album renders as a chip you can tap through.

Compilations and album artists handled properly.

Library / Home

A Home screen made of your own listening.

Recently played, most played, recently added, favourites, On This Day, and shelves built from what you actually return to. Star ratings and favourites sync straight to your server, so they are the same in every other client you use.

Nothing recommended by a stranger.

Library / Stats

Listening stats that stay on your side.

A 24 hour listening clock, a weekday heatmap, and your top artists, albums and songs with a week, month or year switcher. Computed from your own play history, not from a service.

iPhone, iPad and Mac.

On the App Store today

Playback and sound.

Sound / Path

A real audio path.

A selectable high fidelity playback engine, a 10 band equalizer with presets, ReplayGain per track or per album or automatic, and gapless playback for the records that were written to be heard that way.

FLAC, ALAC, MP3, AAC, Opus, Ogg and more.

Sound / Quality

Wi-Fi and cellular, set separately.

Ten tiers, from the original file down to a light Opus stream, chosen independently for Wi-Fi and for mobile data. Auto sends the original at home and something kind to your data plan on the road. Your server does the transcoding, so nothing is re-encoded on the phone.

A codec and bitrate badge tells you what you are actually hearing, and can be turned off.

Sound / Queue

The queue, and what happens when it ends.

Reorder, shuffle, repeat, play next, add to queue, and a sleep timer. AutoMix keeps going when the queue runs out, using similar tracks resolved from your server or from Last.fm and ListenBrainz if you connect them.

Internet radio stations too.

Sound / Lyrics

Lyrics that keep time.

Time-synced LRC lyrics on every platform, scrolling with the song, with a tap on any line to seek there. Plain lyrics render properly when that is all a track has.

Including the Watch.

On the App Store today

Offline, properly.

Offline / Downloads

Pin a song, an album, a playlist, or an entire artist.

A download queue with live speed, a history, and one tap to retry whatever failed. Artwork and metadata are cached with the audio, so the library browses offline as well as plays, which is the part most clients skip.

An LRU cache with a size limit you set.

Offline / Behaviour

Prefer Downloaded Music, and Keep Streamed Songs.

Play the local file even when the server is reachable, so a good connection never costs you data you already spent. Keep Streamed Songs quietly saves what you play, so the music you return to ends up on the device without you managing anything.

Downloads you asked for are never deleted automatically.

One purchase

Every screen Apple makes.

Five native apps plus CarPlay, one $5.99 Universal Purchase. No client in the Navidrome apps directory covers all five.

iPhone

iPhone and CarPlay.

The full library in your pocket, Siri support, Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy widgets, and CarPlay with voice search that understands the play command in all eleven app languages and resolves it against your own library. iPhone page.

iPad

iPad, as an iPad app.

A real iPadOS layout with a sidebar, a Featured Album hero and density tuned for the display, not a phone app stretched to fill the glass. Pointer and keyboard are first class. iPad page.

Mac

Mac, natively.

SwiftUI, not Electron, not Catalyst, not an iPhone binary. A Mini Player that pins on top of any window, a menu bar presence, four desktop widgets, and a 7 day free trial. Mac page.

Apple TV

Apple TV, built for the remote.

Focus navigation done properly, Top Shelf, and an in-app language picker because tvOS offers no per-app language setting at all. Apple TV page.

Apple Watch

Apple Watch, without the phone.

Standalone playback on the wrist, with downloads pulled straight from your server over Wi-Fi and an iPhone transfer as the fallback. A complication, lyrics, and Crown volume. Apple Watch page.

Linux and beyond

Linux now, Android and Windows next.

A native Linux desktop build is out as a free open beta, carrying its own Java and VLC so there is nothing to install. A native Android client is finished and in closed beta, and a Windows client is in active development. Linux page.

Optional, always

What it connects to, if you let it.

Last.fm

Last.fm, routed through your own server.

Listener counts, similar artists, loved track sync, and scrobbles that go out through Navidrome rather than straight from the app. See the integration.

ListenBrainz

ListenBrainz, first class.

The open, self-hostable alternative from the MusicBrainz nonprofit. Your ListenBrainz shelf, Weekly and Daily Jams, two way loved track sync, and a Preferred Source picker where it shares a surface with Last.fm. See the integration.

NaviBeat Mixes

Mixes your own server builds.

A free, open source plugin for Navidrome 0.63.1 and newer that generates up to 23 playlists from how you listen: morning and night mixes, Rediscover, artist and genre radios, decade mixes. They are ordinary server playlists, so every client sees them. Get the plugin.

Rockbox

NaviSyncRock: your Navidrome on a real iPod.

Sync songs, albums, artists and playlists from your server to a Rockbox iPod from a normal Mac app. Your server transcodes, artwork is re-encoded for old hardware, plays on the device sync back, and two NaviBeat iPod themes are installed. A one time $4.99 unlock on the Mac. How it works.

The part that does not change

Privacy, security and languages.

Privacy

Zero analytics. Zero tracking.

There is no NaviBeat backend. The app talks to your server and to nothing else, unless you connect Last.fm or ListenBrainz yourself. No telemetry, no crash pings, no accounts. Privacy policy.

Security

mTLS client certificates.

Import a client certificate and reach a server that refuses anyone without one. No other Apple client here supports them at all. Custom proxy headers ride alongside, for setups behind an identity-aware proxy.

Languages

Eleven languages, and you can force one.

English, German, French, Spanish, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Ukrainian, Serbian, Simplified and Traditional Chinese. An in-app picker overrides the system language, including on Apple TV. Ask for yours.
